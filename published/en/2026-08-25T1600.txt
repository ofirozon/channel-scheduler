🎙️ Ever typed a long prompt you could have said out loud?

Turn on dictation in tap mode:

`/voice tap`

Tap `Space` with an empty prompt box, talk, then tap again. Claude Code inserts the transcript and sends it. A tap that catches fewer than three words gets inserted but not submitted, so a stray tap won't fire off half a sentence.

You'll need a Claude.ai login and a local microphone. An API key session, Bedrock, or SSH can't record.

Worth it because: transcription costs no messages or tokens and doesn't count toward your `/usage` limits.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/voice-dictation
