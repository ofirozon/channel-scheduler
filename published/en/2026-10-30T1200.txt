📊 Your OTel cost dashboard says `custom` is the big spender. That's probably your own subagent, renamed.

Claude Code redacts names on its cost and token counters by default. Your own agents and MCP servers come through as `"custom"`, third-party plugin skills as `"third-party"`. Ask which agent eats the budget and every row says the same word.

Next to `CLAUDE_CODE_ENABLE_TELEMETRY=1`, set:

`OTEL_LOG_TOOL_DETAILS=1`

On v2.1.273 or later, the counters then carry the real names.

It's not only names. The same flag sends full Bash commands and tool input to your collector, so check what it stores before a team-wide rollout.

Then the expensive agent finally has a name.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/monitoring-usage
