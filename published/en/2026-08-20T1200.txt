🕐 Ever hit your usage limit and come back to a dead session?

Claude Code now picks your session back up on its own, the moment your claude.ai limit resets. Nothing to retype, and you don't have to be there when it does.

It's on by default, and that's the part worth a thought: a long unattended run will restart itself in the middle of the night. If you'd rather it stayed stopped, open `/config` and switch off "Continue automatically at usage limit".

Requires v2.1.234 or later.

Worth it because: a limit you hit at 2am costs you the reset window, not the whole night.

Source: Claude Code changelog, v2.1.234
