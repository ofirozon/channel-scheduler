✂️ Renaming a call across 200 files isn't one job. It's twenty small ones that don't overlap.

That's what `/batch` is for. In a git repo:

`/batch migrate src/ from JavaScript to TypeScript`

Claude researches the codebase, splits the change into 5 to 30 independent units and shows you the plan. Nothing runs until you approve it. Then each unit gets a background subagent in its own worktree that implements it, runs the tests and publishes the change.

Read that plan. If two units edit the same file, they weren't independent, and that's what to fix before you say yes.

Twenty small diffs to review, not one you'd never finish.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/commands, code.claude.com/docs/en/agents
