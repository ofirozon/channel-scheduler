🏷️ Your team's Claude-written PRs may already be labelled in GitHub.

On Teams or Enterprise with contribution metrics on (a public beta), every merged PR with lines matched to a Claude Code session gets the label `claude-code-assisted`. So the list is one command away:

`gh pr list --state merged --label claude-code-assisted`

The matching is cautious on purpose. A line you rewrote by more than 20% stops counting, and only sessions from 21 days before to 2 days after the merge are checked.

I'd read the number as a floor, not a score.

When someone asks which PRs Claude touched, you hand them a list instead of a guess.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/analytics
