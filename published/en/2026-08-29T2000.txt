🌳 Sick of every isolated branch copying your whole monorepo to disk?

Tell Claude Code which directories a task actually touches. In `.claude/settings.json`:

`{`
`  "worktree": {`
`    "sparsePaths": [".claude", "packages/api", "packages/shared"],`
`    "symlinkDirectories": ["node_modules"]`
`  }`
`}`

Now a `--worktree` session checks out those directories plus root-level files, and symlinks `node_modules` back to your main checkout instead of duplicating it.

Don't leave `.claude` off that list. Root-level directories aren't checked out on their own, so without it the worktree starts without your repo's own settings, rules and skills.

Worth it because: a worktree in a huge repo starts in seconds on a fraction of the disk.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/large-codebases
