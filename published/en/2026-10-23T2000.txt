🍴 The best subagent is the one you don't have to brief.

Mid-task, type:

`/subtask draft unit tests for the parser changes so far`

That starts a fork. It inherits the whole conversation, same system prompt, tools and model, so you explain nothing. It runs in a panel below your prompt while you keep working, and only its final result comes back.

It's cheaper than it sounds. Its first request reuses your prompt cache, where a fresh subagent builds its own.

Select its row and press `Enter` to send it a follow-up. `x` stops it.

Use it whenever briefing a new subagent would take longer than the task.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/sub-agents#fork-the-current-conversation
