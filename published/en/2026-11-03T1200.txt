🔬 Want ultrareview findings in your CI log? `claude -p` is the wrong door.

`claude -p '/code-review ultra'` starts the cloud review and exits without waiting, so the findings never reach the job. This one waits:

`claude ultrareview 1234 --trust-workspace`

Findings go to stdout, progress to stderr, and it exits 0 once the review completes, findings or not. Failure or timeout is 1, and the default wait is 45 minutes.

CI can't answer the trust prompt, and without the flag the run dies on `Error: Workspace not trusted`. The flag needs v2.1.309 or later. Only pass it on code you'd trust.

Your pipeline gets the findings, not a launch receipt.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/ultrareview
