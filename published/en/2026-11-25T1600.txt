⬆️ The footer says an update installed. Are you really quitting mid-task for it?

You don't have to lose your place. Type:

`/restart`

Claude Code comes back up on the new version and resumes the same session, conversation and all. `/update` is the same command.

It needs v2.1.288 or later, a Pro, Max or Ultra plan, and the native install on macOS or Linux. On WSL it isn't available.

It's easy to leave an update pending for days when restarting means rebuilding context. Now it doesn't.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/commands
