🏷️ Four sessions running in parallel, and tomorrow you need the third one back.

Name it when you start it:

`claude -n auth-refactor`

The name sits on the prompt bar and the terminal tab, and tomorrow you skip the picker:

`claude --resume auth-refactor`

That finds it from any worktree of the same repo. An exact match resumes straight away. If two sessions share the name, the picker opens with it already typed in.

Forgot at launch? `/rename auth-refactor` does the same thing mid-session.

Name it like you'd search for it later. A ticket number beats "fix stuff" every time.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/sessions, code.claude.com/docs/en/settings-reference#terminaltitlefromrename
