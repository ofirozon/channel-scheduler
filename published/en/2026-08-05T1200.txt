☕ Ever come back from lunch, ask one small question, and watch it cost a fortune?

Claude Code resends your whole conversation with every request. Prompt caching usually makes that cheap, but the cache expires: one hour on a subscription, five minutes once you're drawing on usage credits, five minutes by default on an API key.

Come back after a longer break and your first message misses, reprocessing the entire context at full price.

Finish the thought before you step away. After a long break, `/clear` unless you genuinely need the history.

Worth it because: the priciest message of your day is the one right after lunch.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/costs
