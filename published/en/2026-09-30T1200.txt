🎵 Your subagents set to `model: sonnet` get a new model after one `claude update`.

Anthropic released Claude Sonnet 5.5 on 28 September 2026, and from Claude Code v2.1.284 the `sonnet` alias resolves to it on the Anthropic API. Older builds keep resolving it to Sonnet 5. Same for `opusplan`, which executes on Sonnet.

The price didn't move: $2 in, $10 out per million tokens. Anthropic says it writes 30% faster than Sonnet 5 and costs up to 30% less per task, from fewer tokens and tool calls. Their number, so check it on your own jobs.

Pinned `claude-sonnet-5` somewhere? That stays put until you change it.

Source: anthropic.com/claude-sonnet-5-5, code.claude.com/docs/en/model-config
