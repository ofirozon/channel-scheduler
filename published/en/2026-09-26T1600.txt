🪤 That `Write(src/**)` rule in your settings has never been checked. Not once.

File permissions in Claude Code are matched against `Edit(path)` and `Read(path)` rules, and nothing else. Write the same path rule for `Write`, `NotebookEdit` or the legacy `MultiEdit` and it's accepted, saved, and never consulted. You get a startup warning, which is the kind of line everyone scrolls past.

One word fixes it:

`"allow": ["Edit(docs/**)"]`

`Edit` governs writes to those paths too. A bare `Write` with no path is a different thing and does match everywhere, so leave that one alone.

Go look at your own allow list.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/permissions
