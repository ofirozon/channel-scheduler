🗺️ Auto mode keeps blocking pushes to your team's other repos.

It isn't paranoid. By default the classifier trusts your working directory and this repo's remotes, nothing else. Your company's GitHub org and your staging bucket both count as outside.

Skip writing that list by hand. Run:

`/auto-mode-setup`

It asks how you use the project, then reads your CLAUDE.md, config files, git remotes and the hosts your recent sessions touched, never your messages, and drafts `autoMode.environment` entries for `~/.claude/settings.json`.

Read the draft before you accept it. Whatever it lists, the classifier stops treating as a possible leak.

Then the blocks you get are the ones worth stopping for.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/auto-mode-config
