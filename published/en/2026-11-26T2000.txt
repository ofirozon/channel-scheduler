🔁 Made a new skills folder mid-session, and Claude acts like it isn't there?

Claude Code watches `~/.claude/skills/` and your project's `.claude/skills/` while you work, so adding or editing a skill there just shows up. A skills directory that didn't exist when the session started isn't being watched. Run:

`/reload-skills`

It re-scans and tells you how many skills are available and how many were added or removed. That folder still isn't watched afterwards, so run it again after each change you make there.

Beats restarting the session and throwing away everything in context.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/skills (Edit a skill during a session), code.claude.com/docs/en/commands
