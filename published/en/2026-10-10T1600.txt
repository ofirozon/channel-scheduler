🧩 Skills you switched on at claude.ai follow you into the terminal.

Sign in with `/login` and Claude Code downloads them into `~/.claude/skills/synced/`. Some of Anthropic's own, like `pdf` and `xlsx`, come along whether you switched them on or not. Run `/skills` and they're listed under `claude.ai sync`.

Don't fix one in place. The sync only goes down, so your edit never reaches claude.ai and the next sync can overwrite it. Change it on claude.ai instead.

If you'd rather keep this machine clean, set `"syncClaudeAiSkills": false` in `~/.claude/settings.json`.

Thirty seconds in `/skills` tells you what Claude can already reach for.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/skills
