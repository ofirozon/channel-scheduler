🎹 Reaching for Esc a few hundred times a day is vim mode's worst part.

If your editor already maps `jj` to Escape, Claude Code's prompt can too. In `~/.claude/settings.json`:

`"editorMode": "vim",`
`"vimInsertModeRemaps": { "jj": "<Esc>" }`

Hit both keys within a second and you're in NORMAL mode, with neither `j` left in your prompt. Pause between them and both stay as text.

It has to be your user settings. A project's `.claude/settings.json` is ignored for this one, so a repo you clone can't remap your keys.

Same fingers in the editor and the prompt box, no translating.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/interactive-mode
