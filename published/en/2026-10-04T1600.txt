🚦 Auto mode will push to your default branch without asking. That's the default.

The classifier still stops a force push or a secret heading into a commit. An ordinary `git push` to main goes straight through, and so does `gh pr create`.

To see those first, put ask rules under `permissions` in your settings:

`"ask": ["Bash(git push *)", "Bash(gh pr create *)"]`

Ask rules are checked before the classifier, so they prompt even in auto mode.

They match on how the command starts. `git -C repo push` slips past, and catching that takes a PreToolUse hook.

I'd rather click once per push than learn about it from CI.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/auto-mode-config
