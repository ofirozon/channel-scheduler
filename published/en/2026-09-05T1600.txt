📤 Someone asks what Claude just did, and you reach for a screenshot.

There's a command for that. From inside the session:

`/export handoff.txt`

It writes the whole conversation as plain text, messages and tool outputs rendered readably. With no filename it opens a menu, and copying to your clipboard is one of the options.

Don't go digging for the raw file instead. Transcripts sit in `~/.claude/projects/` as JSONL, but that entry format is internal to Claude Code and changes between releases, so anything you build on it breaks on the next update.

Worth it because: a reviewable record of a session costs one command, not fourteen screenshots.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/sessions
