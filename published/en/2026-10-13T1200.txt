⏱️ Your CI job spends its first 30 seconds waiting on a server that never connects.

In a headless `claude -p` run, the first turn waits for MCP servers that are still connecting. A stdio server that hangs holds it to `MCP_TIMEOUT`, 30 seconds by default, on every run.

From v2.1.274 you can set that wait:

`CLAUDE_CODE_MCP_STARTUP_WAIT_MS=0 claude -p "..."`

`0` skips it. A number in milliseconds caps it for every pending server.

If the job's first step needs that server's tools, I'd give it a short cap like `5000` instead of zero.

A flaky server stops costing you half a minute per run.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/env-vars and /headless
