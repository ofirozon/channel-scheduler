🕒 `/usage` says `rate limited until 3:14am`. Your session is fine.

That line is about the usage page, not about you. Claude Code couldn't fetch your plan limits, most often because the usage endpoint itself is rate limited, and it can't ask again until that time. Your work with Claude isn't limited by it.

If it loaded your bars on this machine in the past 60 minutes, you get those instead, with a `Showing last-known usage` note saying how old they are.

I'd check that note's age before stopping a task halfway out of panic.
