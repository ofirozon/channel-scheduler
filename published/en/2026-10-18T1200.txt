📄 Point Claude at a huge generated file and you'll often watch it read the thing in pages.

That's the default. One Read call returns at most 25,000 tokens and refuses a whole file over 256 KB, so Claude walks through it with `offset` and `limit`.

Since v2.1.296 it can take the whole file in one call when it needs to. So ask:

`Read all of src/legacy/billing.ts in one go, the entire file, before you change anything.`

That read is sized against the room left in your context, so do it early in a session, not two hours in.

One read, and Claude sees the file as one piece instead of stitched pages.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/tools-reference
