📉 19% of novice sessions that hit trouble end abandoned, with no code written. For everyone else it's 5 to 7%.

That's from Anthropic's study of about 400,000 Claude Code sessions. Expertise there isn't years of coding, it's rated per task on three signals: how precisely you frame the ask, what you ask Claude to verify, and whether you're the one catching its mistakes.

The middle one is the easiest to borrow. Say what done looks like before you start: the test that has to pass, the output you'll check.

Most of the gap sits between novice and intermediate, so a working grasp gets you nearly all of it.
