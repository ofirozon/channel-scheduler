⏱️ You hit `/exit` and your cleanup hook dies mid-upload.

`SessionEnd` hooks get 1.5 seconds by default, all of them together. That covers exit, `/clear`, and switching sessions with `/resume`.

Give the slow hook its own limit, in seconds:

`"timeout": 10`

The shared budget rises to match the highest per-hook `timeout` in your settings, up to 60 seconds. Hooks without one still get 1.5.

Timeouts on plugin hooks don't raise it. For those, set the budget when you launch:

`CLAUDE_CODE_SESSIONEND_HOOKS_TIMEOUT_MS=5000 claude`

Ten seconds of config, and the log you meant to save actually gets saved.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/hooks#sessionend
