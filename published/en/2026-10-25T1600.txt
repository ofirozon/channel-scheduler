🎯 You want one commit reviewed, not the whole branch.

Type the target after the command:

`/code-review abc1234^..abc1234`

That's a single commit. The same slot takes a range, a branch comparison, a directory or a PR number:

`/code-review HEAD~15..HEAD`
`/code-review main...my-feature`
`/code-review src/auth`
`/code-review 1234`

With no target, it reviews your commits ahead of upstream plus whatever's uncommitted, and compares against `main` when there's no upstream.

The single-commit form is the one people miss. It's how you get a second look at the hotfix that went in last night without dragging the rest of the branch into the review.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/code-review-local
