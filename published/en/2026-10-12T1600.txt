🔑 Your commit skill ran git without one prompt. You replied "now commit the rest" and the prompts were back.

That's how `allowed-tools` in a skill's frontmatter works:

`allowed-tools: Bash(git add *) Bash(git commit *) Bash(git status *)`

It pre-approves those tools only for the turn that invoked the skill. The grant clears with your next message. The skill's instructions stay in context, its permissions don't.

Invoke the skill again rather than chatting with it, and the grant comes back for that turn. For git approved all session, use an allow rule in your permission settings.

Once you know it's per turn, the second prompt stops looking like a bug.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/skills
