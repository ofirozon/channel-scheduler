🧩 Still keeping `.claude/commands/` and `.claude/skills/` straight in your head?

You don't have to. A file at `.claude/commands/deploy.md` and a skill at `.claude/skills/deploy/SKILL.md` both give you /deploy, and they behave the same. Your existing command files keep working, same frontmatter, nothing to migrate today.

One rule to remember: if a skill and a command share a name, the skill wins.

Write the next one as a skill and you also get a directory for supporting files, plus the option to let Claude load it on its own when it's relevant.

Worth it because: one concept to learn instead of two.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/skills
