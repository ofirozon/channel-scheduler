👀 You don't need a second terminal to watch your dev server's log.

Ask Claude:

`Tail logs/dev.log and tell me when an error shows up`

That's the Monitor tool. Claude runs a small script in the background and gets each new line as it lands, so it can interject while you keep working. CI jobs and folders work too. On Bedrock, Vertex or Foundry it needs v2.1.310 or later.

The surprise is the clock. A watch ends after 5 minutes by default, 30 at most, and Claude gets one notice when it does. Want it watching all afternoon? Say so, and it can start the watch again.

One less window to babysit.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/tools-reference#monitor-tool, Claude Code CHANGELOG v2.1.310
