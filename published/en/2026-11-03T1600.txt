🔗 Claude prints `PROJ-1234` and you copy it into the browser. It could just be a link.

In your user `~/.claude/settings.json` (project settings don't take this key):

```
"footerLinksRegexes": [{
  "type": "regex",
  "pattern": "\\b(?<key>PROJ-\\d+)\\b",
  "url": "https://issues.example.com/browse/{key}"
}]
```

Now every `PROJ-1234` in a tool result or in Claude's reply becomes a clickable badge below the input box. The named group fills `{key}`, and up to five badges show at once.

Mind the regex. It runs on the main thread after every turn, so a nested quantifier like `(a+)+` can freeze the session. Keep it linear.

Ticket numbers stop being something you retype.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/settings-reference#footerlinksregexes
