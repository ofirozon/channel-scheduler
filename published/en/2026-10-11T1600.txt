🧾 Your agent asked the user a question. The user went home.

In an Agent SDK app, a `canUseTool` callback can stay pending forever, so your process has to stay alive and hold the question open all night.

For answers that take hours, have a `PreToolUse` hook return

`permissionDecision: "defer"`

The run exits with `stop_reason: "tool_deferred"`, and the pending call, question included, comes back in `deferred_tool_use`. When the answer arrives, resume the session and the hook returns `allow` with it in `updatedInput`.

It only works when Claude made a single tool call in that turn.

Your worker can restart overnight and the question is still there in the morning.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/agent-sdk/user-input and /hooks
