⛓️ Typing one slash command, waiting for it, then typing the next one?

Stack them in a single message:

/write-tests /fix-issue 123

Both skills load, and the trailing `123` reaches each of them as $ARGUMENTS. Claude Code expands the first skill plus up to five more after it.

Expansion stops at the first token that isn't an inline skill, and everything from there becomes argument text. You need v2.1.199 or later; before that only the first skill loaded and the rest arrived as literal text.

Worth it because: a two step routine becomes one line.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/skills
