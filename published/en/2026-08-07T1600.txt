📎 Is your CLAUDE.md a 400-line wall nobody wants to edit?

Split it. A CLAUDE.md can pull other files in with `@path/to/import`, anywhere in the text:

`- git workflow @docs/git-instructions.md`

Relative paths resolve from the file doing the importing, not from your working directory, and imports nest up to four hops deep.

Don't expect a smaller context, though. Imported files still load at launch, so what you're buying is organization, not tokens. And if you want to name a path without importing it, wrap it in backticks.

Worth it because: five short topic files stay current. One 400-line wall doesn't.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/memory
