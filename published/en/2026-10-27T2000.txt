🧾 The usage tips you keep reading and ignoring now take one key.

On a Claude plan, `/usage` lists recommendations, one per row:

`25%  Trim ~/code/api/CLAUDE.md · 49k tokens per request`

Since v2.1.305 the footer can show a hint like `f to fix 2 of 3`. Press `f`. Rows ending in an `/effort` command get run for you. For the others it can handle, Claude gets one prompt and shows you each change before making it. `run /compact` rows stay yours to type.

No hint? Remote Control is connected. Disconnect, then reopen `/usage`.

Shortest path I know from reading the advice to taking it.
