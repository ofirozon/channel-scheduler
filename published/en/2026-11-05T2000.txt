🗄️ That session from five weeks ago isn't in `/resume`. Claude Code deleted it, and it didn't tell you.

Transcripts are kept 30 days by default. A background sweep after a session starts removes anything older, quietly, and plan files, file-history snapshots and debug logs go with them.

Want longer? In `~/.claude/settings.json`:

`"cleanupPeriodDays": 365`

`0` doesn't mean forever. It fails validation, so pick a big number. If you go really big, be on v2.1.310 or later: before that, a huge value could make the sweep delete recent files like pasted images.

A year costs some disk and keeps the session you'll want to dig up in March.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/settings-reference, Claude Code CHANGELOG v2.1.310
