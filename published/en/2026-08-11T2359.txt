💤 Closing the laptop shouldn't kill a twenty-minute refactor.

Hand the task to a cloud VM instead:

    claude --cloud "Fix the flaky test in auth.spec.ts"

It clones your repo from GitHub at your current branch, so push first: the VM pulls from the remote, not your disk. Then it keeps going while you're on a train, and you can watch it from the Claude mobile app.

When you want it back on your machine, `claude --teleport` fetches the session's branch and the full conversation into your terminal.

Research preview, on Pro, Max and Team.

Worth it because: long tasks stop being hostage to your laptop lid.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/claude-code-on-the-web
