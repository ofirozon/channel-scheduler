🏢 Works at home, dies on the office Wi-Fi with `UNABLE_TO_GET_ISSUER_CERT_LOCALLY`.

That's your company's proxy opening HTTPS traffic and re-signing it with its own certificate, one Claude Code doesn't trust. Ask IT for the root CA as a PEM file, then add this to your shell profile:

`export NODE_EXTRA_CA_CERTS="$HOME/certs/corp-root.pem"`

Claude Code reads it once at startup, so restart the session after.

Whatever a forum thread tells you, don't set `NODE_TLS_REJECT_UNAUTHORIZED=0`. That doesn't fix trust. It switches certificate checking off for everything.

One file, and you stop thinking about the proxy.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/network-config, code.claude.com/docs/en/errors (SSL certificate errors)
