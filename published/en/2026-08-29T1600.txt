🔍 Your terminal's search stopped finding anything in the conversation?

That's fullscreen rendering. It's drawing on the alternate screen buffer the way vim does, so nothing lands in your terminal's real scrollback and `Cmd+f` has nothing to look at.

Two keys hand it back:

`Ctrl+o` then `[`

That writes the whole conversation into native scrollback with tool output expanded, so `Cmd+f` and tmux copy mode can see it again.

It lasts until you leave transcript mode with `Esc` or `q`, and the next `Ctrl+o` starts fresh.

Worth it because: you can search back through a long session instead of re-running the command that printed the error.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/fullscreen
