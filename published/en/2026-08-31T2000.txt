⏪ Spent an hour on an approach you now want to abandon?

`/compact` is the wrong reflex. It spends a model call summarizing the mess, then keeps it.

Run this instead, or press `Esc` twice on an empty prompt:

`/rewind`

Pick the message from before the detour. Your original prompt lands back in the input box, ready to edit.

The next turn's quick: you land on history the cache already holds, even past the cache lifetime.

"Restore conversation" keeps the code Claude wrote. The option above it drops that too.

Worth it because: walking back a bad hour costs two keystrokes, not a summarization call.
