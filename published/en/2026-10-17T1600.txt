⏳ The script printed Claude's answer, then just sat there.

A `claude -p` run stays open while background work Claude started is still going: a dev server, a subagent, a Monitor watch.

To see what it's waiting on, run the same command by hand in a terminal. Since v2.1.295, after five seconds it prints a line to stderr starting with

`Waiting for background work to finish`

and names the work. Under cron or CI stderr isn't a terminal, which is why you never saw it there.

Now you fix the one thing holding the run open, instead of wrapping the whole job in `timeout`.
