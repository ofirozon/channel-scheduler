🔬 Claude found a plausible cause for your bug, and now every check it runs seems to agree.

That's anchoring. One agent tends to stop at the first good theory. With agent teams turned on (`CLAUDE_CODE_EXPERIMENTAL_AGENT_TEAMS=1`, still experimental), make them argue:

`Users report the app exits after one message. Spawn 5 agent teammates to investigate different hypotheses. Have them try to disprove each other's theories, like a scientific debate. Update the findings doc with whatever consensus emerges.`

Each teammate is a full Claude instance with its own bill, so I'd save this for the bug that's beaten you once.

The theory still standing after five skeptics is usually the real one.
