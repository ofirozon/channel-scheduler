🔔 Your migration runs in another terminal, and you keep tabbing over to check it.

Ask from the session you're in:

`Tell me when the migration session finishes what it's working on`

Claude subscribes to that session and gets one notice when it next goes idle or exits. The notice can carry a one-line status from that turn. Asking on its own starts no turn and spends no tokens in the session you're watching.

Both sessions have to be on this machine, on v2.1.236 or later. If no notice comes within 12 hours, Claude Code drops the subscription and tells Claude.

You get told when it's done instead of checking.
