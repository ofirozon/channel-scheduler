🧪 Your plugin passed every eval. That doesn't prove it did anything.

`claude plugin eval` runs each case twice, once with your plugin loaded and once with no plugin at all. From the plugin root:

`claude plugin eval .`

You get `WITH`, `W/OUT` and `Δ`, the gap. A case at 1.0 in both columns passes on Claude alone.

The usual first surprise is a `Δ` near zero because Claude never picked your skill. That's a `description` problem, not a code problem.

Every run is a real model call on your account, three per arm by default.

Now you know which parts earn their place.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/plugin-evals
