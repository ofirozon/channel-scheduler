🪄 Your `/btw` answer says Claude hasn't read that file yet.

Side questions get no tools, so normally that's where it ends. Look at the overlay, though. Press `f` on the answer.

That forks a background subagent carrying the whole conversation plus your question and its answer, and this one can read files and run commands. It sits in a panel below your prompt while you keep working, and only its final result comes back into your conversation.

Local sessions only.

You don't retype the question, and the file reads stay out of your main context.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/interactive-mode and /sub-agents
