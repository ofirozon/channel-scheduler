🔒 How often do you approve a domain you've never heard of?

Sandboxed commands prompt for each new host, and three hours in you wave through things you wouldn't have at the start. You can take the choice away. In `~/.claude/settings.json`:

`"sandbox": { "enabled": true, "network": { "strictAllowlist": true, "allowedDomains": ["github.com", "*.npmjs.org"] } }`

Anything off that list is denied outright now, no prompt to click through. One trap: this counts only from your user settings, managed settings, or `--settings`. A repo's own `.claude/settings.json` has no effect, so set it in yours or nothing changes.

Worth it because: your allowlist stays the one you wrote when you were paying attention.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/sandboxing
