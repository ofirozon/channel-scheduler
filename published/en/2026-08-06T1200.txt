📊 You know you burn a lot of tokens. Do you know where?

`/usage` breaks your recent usage down by skill, subagent, plugin and individual MCP server, each as a percentage of the total. Press `d` or `w` to switch between the last 24 hours and the last 7 days. It's on the Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise plans.

It also flags any behavior that accounts for 10% or more, like long context or cache misses, and gives you a tip for each.

Worth it because: "I burn a lot of tokens" becomes "one MCP server is 30% of my week", and that you can act on today.
