📝 Claude writes a long reply, you hit `Ctrl+G` to answer in your editor, and the reply you're answering is gone from view.

Turn on Show last response in external editor in `/config`. Or set it in `~/.claude.json`:

`"externalEditorContext": true`

Now the editor opens with Claude's last response at the top as `#` comment lines, and you write under the marker. Claude Code strips the comments when you save, so only your text gets sent. A long response keeps its last 50 lines.

Put the key in `settings.json` and nothing happens. Claude Code ignores it there, and that's the mistake I'd expect most people to make.

Answering point three no longer means remembering what point three said.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/settings-reference#externaleditorcontext
