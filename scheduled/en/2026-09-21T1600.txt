🪟 Still opening a diff viewer that hides the conversation behind it?

In fullscreen rendering, `/diff` opens a panel beside the conversation instead:

`/diff`

It lists the changed files with their added and removed line counts, and refreshes every time Claude edits a file or runs a shell command. Select lines in it with the mouse and they ride along with your next prompt.

One requirement catches people out: the terminal has to be at least 110 columns wide. Narrower than that and you're back to the old viewer.

You review edits as they land, without losing your place in the conversation.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/interactive-mode#diff-panel
