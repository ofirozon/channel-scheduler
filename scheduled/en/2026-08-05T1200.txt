You came back from lunch, asked one small question, and it cost you a lot.

Claude Code sends your whole conversation with every request. Prompt caching normally makes that cheap, but the cache has a lifetime: one hour on a subscription, five minutes once you are drawing on usage credits, and five minutes by default on an API key or a cloud provider.

Come back after a longer break and your first message misses the cache and reprocesses the entire context at full price.

So before you step away from a big session, finish the thought. After a long break, /clear and start clean unless you truly need the history.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/costs
