🔁 Ever needed one more answer from a subagent that already finished?

A subagent that succeeds drops out of `/tasks` about 30 seconds later. Since v2.1.319 there's a way back. Run:

`/tasks`

and open the "Earlier in this session" row. It lists finished subagents. Pick one and message it, even after a restart, once you resume the session. It keeps its full history, so "now check the auth module too" costs one line instead of a fresh agent rereading your repo.

Explore and Plan are one-shot and can't be resumed. For work you'll want to continue, use general-purpose or a custom subagent.

Source: Claude Code changelog v2.1.319, code.claude.com/docs/en/sub-agents
