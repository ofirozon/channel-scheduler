🧯 A headless run loops on a flaky test. Who stops it?

`claude -p` has no turn limit by default, so a job that keeps almost-fixing something keeps going, and keeps spending. Put a ceiling on it:

`claude -p --max-turns 3 "fix the failing test"`

Hitting that ceiling isn't a quiet stop. The run exits with an error, so your CI step goes red instead of reporting success on work that never finished.

It's print mode only, so there's nothing to undo for your interactive sessions.

Worth it because: a runaway agent costs you three turns instead of an afternoon.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/cli-reference
