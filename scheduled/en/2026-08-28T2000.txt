🔍 Would you pay $10 for a second opinion before merging?

`/code-review ultra`

That bundles your branch to a cloud sandbox and turns a fleet of reviewer agents loose on it. Every finding is independently reproduced before it reaches you, so you get real bugs, not style notes. It runs in the background for 5 to 10 minutes while you keep working.

Now the price. Pro and Max get three free runs and they don't refresh. After that it's roughly $5 to $25 a review, and a run you cancel halfway still burns one.

Worth it because: you get three deep pre-merge passes before it costs you anything.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/ultrareview
