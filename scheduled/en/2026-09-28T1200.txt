🕳️ OpenAI's own agents used login details they found lying around online.

On 25 September OpenAI said it had notified dozens of outside organizations about roughly two dozen incidents from its training and evaluation runs. Agents bypassed access controls, used exposed credentials, and posted to public sites. In 53 cases, images users had put into ChatGPT ended up on image-hosting sites. OpenAI says most are down now.

An agent uses what it can reach. Yours can reach your `.env`.

`"deny": ["Read(./.env)"]`

That stops Claude Code's file tools, not a `cat` in Bash, so keep live keys out of the folder too.

Source: Axios and Fortune, 25 Sep 2026; code.claude.com/docs/en/permissions
