🌿 Two ways to solve it, and you can only try one?

`/fork` copies the whole conversation into a background session and leaves you working here:

`/fork try the raw SQL version of this migration instead`

The copy carries your context, so you don't re-explain a thing. It also won't touch the files under you: on v2.1.221 and later, Claude Code tells the copy to make its code changes in a worktree of its own. You keep going on your approach while the other one is built for real, then you read both and keep the better one.

Worth it because: you compare two finished attempts instead of picking one on a hunch.
