🔎 `/code-review` stops at a usual limit of findings, and on a big branch that limit decides what you get to read.

Since v2.1.288 you can move it:

`/code-review high --max-findings all`

`all` reports every finding. A number works too, like `--max-findings 20`. Pair it with `high`, because at `low` and `medium` the review only keeps what it's most confident in.

The flag sticks. Later reviews reuse your value until you type `--max-findings default`, so the cap you set for one huge branch follows you into the next two-line fix.

Use it before a merge you're nervous about. For everyday diffs the short list is the useful one.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/code-review
