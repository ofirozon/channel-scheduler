⏰ Hit your usage limit halfway through a refactor at 11pm? Don't close the terminal.

On a claude.ai subscription, Claude Code waits in the open session and picks the task back up once the limit resets. The bottom of the screen says when:

`Continuing automatically at 3:45pm · Esc to cancel`

It's on by default. Exiting, `/clear` or `/resume` ends the wait, and so does typing a new prompt. It still asks for permissions too, so one approval can park it until you're back.

If you want nothing running unattended, turn off Continue automatically at usage limit in `/config`.

Otherwise leave it open, and the refactor's further along by morning.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/interactive-mode
