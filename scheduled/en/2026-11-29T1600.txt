🧵 Claude's subagents start in the background by default. Here's how to stay in charge of one.

1. Ask for one in plain words:
`use a subagent to find every test in src/ that sleeps instead of waiting`
It shows up in a panel below your prompt. Keep working.
2. When it needs permission, the prompt lands in your session with the subagent's name on it. Esc denies that one tool call, and the subagent carries on.
3. Think twice before answering one of those with a grant for the rest of the session. It applies to the whole session, your main conversation included.
4. Don't bother polling. Its result arrives as a notification in a later turn, and asking early just gets you "still running".
5. Want everything stopped? Press `Ctrl+X Ctrl+K` twice within 3 seconds. That ends every running background subagent in the session, even with one of their permission prompts open.
6. Rather have Claude wait for a subagent? Add this to `settings.json` and restart `claude`:
`"env": { "CLAUDE_CODE_FORK_SUBAGENT": "0" }`
Then ask it to "run the reviewer subagent and wait for its report".

Learn step 5 before the day you need it.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/sub-agents (Run subagents in foreground or background), code.claude.com/docs/en/interactive-mode
