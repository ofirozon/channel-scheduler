🩹 Six review findings, and you're pasting them back one at a time?

`/code-review --fix` applies what the review found to your working tree the moment it finishes, instead of handing you a list:

`/code-review high --fix`

It runs as a background subagent with its own context window, so your conversation stays clean while it works.

The catch: a background review's edits land outside your session's checkpoints, so `/rewind` won't undo them. Commit first, and use git if you don't like the result.

Worth it because: the trivial findings are fixed before you've finished reading the list.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/code-review
