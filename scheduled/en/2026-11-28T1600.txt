✏️ Added a rule to CLAUDE.md mid-task, and Claude still breaks it?

It isn't ignoring you. Your project-root and user-level CLAUDE.md are read when the session starts and kept in memory, so an edit you make halfway through never reaches the conversation you're in. To pick it up, run:

`/compact`

That reloads the files and keeps a summary of where you were. `/clear` or a restart works too, if you're fine starting over.

There's an upside to the delay. The edit doesn't touch the prompt cache, so you can tweak the file mid-task and reload once, when it suits you.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/prompt-caching (Editing CLAUDE.md mid-session)
