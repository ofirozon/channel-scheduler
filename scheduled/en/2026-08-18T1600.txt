Four phases, from Anthropic's own best-practices guide. Try it on your next change that touches more than one file.

1. Explore. Press `Shift+Tab` until the status bar shows `⏸ plan mode on`. Claude reads and answers, but can't edit anything.

`read /src/auth and understand how we handle sessions and login.`

2. Plan. Still in plan mode, ask for the plan itself:

`I want to add Google OAuth. What files need to change? What's the session flow? Create a plan.`

Press `Ctrl+G` to open that plan in your editor and fix it by hand before a single line runs.

3. Implement. Approve the plan or press `Shift+Tab` to leave plan mode:

`implement the OAuth flow from your plan. write tests for the callback handler, run the test suite and fix any failures.`

4. Commit.

`commit with a descriptive message and open a PR`

Skip all of it when the scope is obvious. Planning a typo fix is pure overhead: if you could describe the diff in one sentence, just ask for the diff.

Worth it because: rejecting a bad plan takes 30 seconds, rejecting a bad implementation takes the afternoon.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/best-practices
