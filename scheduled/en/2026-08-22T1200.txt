🧳 Coming from Codex or Gemini CLI and dreading the setup again?

Don't retype any of it. Claude Code reads the other tool's config straight off your machine:

`/import codex`

It finds instruction files, MCP servers, commands, subagents and skills, then hands you a picker so you choose what actually comes over. `/import gemini` does the same for Gemini CLI.

Not sure what it will touch? Run `/import codex --dry-run` first and nothing gets written.

Worth it because: the setup you spent months tuning follows you in one command instead of one evening.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/commands
