🌐 OpenAI launched Dots on September 29, 2026, an always-on agent that goes off and finishes multi-step work on its own. Type dot.com looking for it and you land on the download page for Grok, xAI's chatbot.

Whois says the domain moved to xAI in July, roughly two months before the launch rather than after it. No price has been confirmed by either side, and the figures going around are estimates. The innocent reading is typo traffic from people meaning bot, except xAI doesn't own bot.com or vot.com, which weakens it.

A product name is also a domain. Check who holds it before you announce one.
