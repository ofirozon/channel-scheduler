🗑️ You deleted the client repo. Every session you ran in it is still in `~/.claude`.

Transcripts, auto memory, file-edit history, the prompts you typed. One command clears all of it for that project. Look first:

`claude purge ~/work/client-repo --dry-run`

That prints each item it would delete and why. Drop `--dry-run` and it asks once before removing anything.

It also deletes the project's entry in `~/.claude.json`, which is where its trust decision and any local-scope MCP servers live. Purge a repo you still use and you'll be setting those up again.

Worth running before you hand back a work laptop or close out a contract.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/claude-directory (Clear local data)
