🔗 Yesterday's session published a page. Where did the link go?

Claude Code can publish a session's output as a live page on claude.ai, an artifact. The link prints once and scrolls away, and tomorrow's session, which can't see it, publishes a second page instead of updating the first.

`/artifacts`

lists every one you own. Press `o` to open it in the browser, `c` to copy the link, `Enter` to attach it to the session you're in, so the next change updates the page you already shared. Needs v2.1.216 or later.

Worth it because: the link you sent your team keeps pointing at the current version.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/artifacts
