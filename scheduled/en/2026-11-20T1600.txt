🎨 Three Claude Code sessions open, and you just typed into the wrong repo.

Give each one its own prompt bar color. In the session that touches production, run:

`/color red`

You get red, blue, green, yellow, purple, orange, pink and cyan. `/color default` resets it, and no argument picks a random one. It only holds for the current session, so set it when you open the window that matters.

Sounds cosmetic. It's also the cheapest guard there is against approving the right command in the wrong terminal.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/commands
