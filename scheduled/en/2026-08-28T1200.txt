👀 Three background sessions, and no idea which one is stuck?

Run `claude agents` for a full-terminal view of every session. Highlight a row and press `Space`: the peek panel shows the exact question a blocked session is asking, and how long it's been waiting.

Answer right there. Type and hit `Enter`, press a number key when it offers choices, or `Tab` to fill in a suggested reply you can edit first.

Before you fan out, though: each session bills your quota separately, so ten in parallel burn it roughly ten times as fast.

Worth it because: unblocking a stuck agent costs a keypress, not a session switch.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/agent-view
