🏃 Tired of Claude checking in on decisions you'd have made either way?

There's a built-in output style for that. Run `/config`, open Output style, pick Proactive. Claude executes immediately, makes reasonable assumptions on routine calls, and prefers action over planning.

Or set it in a settings file:

`"outputStyle": "Proactive"`

One thing it doesn't touch: permissions. It's guidance to the model, so your permission mode still decides what runs without asking you.

Switch mid-session and it applies from your next message, no `/clear` needed, from v2.1.251.

Worth it because: fewer "would you like me to" turns, with your approvals untouched.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/output-styles
