📋 You can copy one paragraph of Claude's answer, not the whole thing.

When the last response has code blocks or blockquotes in it, `/copy` opens a picker. Pick one block, or the full response. Since v2.1.295 quoted text shows up there too, and it copies without the `>` markers.

That makes drafting easy. Ask for the reply to your reviewer as a blockquote, then:

`/copy`

Pick the quote and paste it straight into GitHub. `/copy 2` reaches one response further back, and over SSH, `w` in the picker writes your pick to a file instead.

No stray `>` at the start of every line to clean up by hand.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/commands
