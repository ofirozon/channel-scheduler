In the project, run:

`/fewer-permission-prompts`

It reads your recent transcripts, picks out the read-only Bash and MCP calls you keep saying yes to, and proposes a prioritized allowlist for `.claude/settings.json`.

That's the project file, usually the one your team commits. Read what it adds before you push, and cut anything you wouldn't want auto-approved on someone else's machine.

It only looks at read-only calls, so I'd try it before switching to a looser permission mode.

The prompts you keep are the ones worth reading.
