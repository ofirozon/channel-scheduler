🗜️ I'd rather pick what survives compaction than leave it to the summary.

A `PreCompact` hook lets you. When it exits 0, whatever it prints becomes instructions for the compaction summary. In `.claude/settings.json`:

`"hooks": {"PreCompact": [{"hooks": [{"type": "command", "command": "cat \"$CLAUDE_PROJECT_DIR\"/.claude/compact-notes.md"}]}]}`

Then keep that file current: the test that's still failing, the approach you already ruled out, the decision you don't want relitigated. With no matcher it runs for `/compact` and auto-compact alike.

Stay under 10,000 characters. Anything past that gets cut, with a marker where it stopped.

It's the stuff you'd otherwise retype after every compaction.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/hooks (PreCompact)
