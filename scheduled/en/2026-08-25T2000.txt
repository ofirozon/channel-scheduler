🐙 That issue nobody on your team has time for?

Let Claude take it from inside GitHub. In the repo, run:

`/install-github-app`

It installs the GitHub App, saves your credential as a repo secret and prepares a pull request with the workflow file. Merge that, then comment on the issue:

`@claude implement this feature based on the issue description`

Claude works in the thread and pushes a branch.

The part that trips people up: whoever comments needs write access to the repo, so a drive-by contributor's `@claude` won't do anything.

Worth it because: an issue becomes a reviewable PR while you're doing something else.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/github-actions
