⏸️ Leave a big session alone for an hour and your next message pays to re-read all of it.

That's the one-hour prompt cache running out. Since v2.1.299, Claude Code gets there first. Once a terminal conversation is past 200,000 tokens and has sat idle about 54 minutes, it compacts, and you come back to

`Compacted while idle, before the prompt cache expired`

Your machine has to be awake at that point, though. Close the lid and nothing happens.

I'd leave it on. If you'd rather keep the full history and pay for the re-read, put `"idleCompaction": false` in settings.json.

Lunch stops costing you the whole session.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/settings-reference#idlecompaction
