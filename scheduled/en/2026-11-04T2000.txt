The Learning output style splits the work. Claude writes the routine parts, and when it hits a real design call, like error handling or business logic with more than one right answer, it leaves a `TODO(human)` in the file and stops.

`/output-style Learning`

You get a short note: what's built, what to write, what to weigh. Write those lines, tell Claude you're done, and it comments on your code and carries on.

Setting it in a file instead? `"outputStyle": "Learning"` works. Lowercase `learning` gives you the default style.

The task still ships, and the hard ten lines are yours.
