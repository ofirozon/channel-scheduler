🔎 Certain you had that session yesterday, and now it's nowhere?

It's there. `/resume` only lists sessions from the worktree you're standing in.

From inside the picker:

`Ctrl+W` widens to every worktree of this repo
`Ctrl+A` widens to every project on this machine
`Ctrl+B` narrows to your current git branch

Each one toggles, so press it again to go back.

The part that catches people out: pick a session from an unrelated project and Claude Code doesn't open it. It copies a `cd` and resume command to your clipboard instead.

Worth it because: the session you thought you lost is two keystrokes away, not a re-explanation away.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/sessions
