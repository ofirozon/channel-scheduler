🧠 Renaming a variable shouldn't cost you thousands of thinking tokens.

Extended thinking is on by default, and thinking tokens bill as output. That's the right call for planning and hard debugging. It's pure waste on mechanical edits, where the default budget can still run to tens of thousands of tokens per request.

Cheapest dial first: drop the effort level with `/effort` or inside `/model`. Turn thinking off entirely in `/config`.

One catch: Fable 5 always thinks, so there's nothing to switch off there.

Worth it because: you stop paying reasoning prices for find and replace.
