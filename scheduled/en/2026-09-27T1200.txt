🐳 `excludedCommands` is the sandbox setting most likely to be wider than you meant.

It's the standard fix for a tool that won't run under Claude Code's Bash sandbox, `docker` being the usual one:

`"sandbox": { "excludedCommands": ["docker *"] }`

The rule underneath is coarser than that entry looks. When any part of a compound command matches, the whole line runs unsandboxed, so `docker ps | ./parse.sh` takes your script out of the sandbox with it.

I'd keep that list short. When a tool only needs to write somewhere, `filesystem.allowWrite` grants the path and leaves the command inside the sandbox.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/settings-reference
