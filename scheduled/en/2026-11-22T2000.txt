⏳ That usage-limit message mid-task doesn't mean the task is dead.

On a claude.ai subscription, Claude Code (v2.1.234+) waits in the open session and picks the task up on its own once the limit resets. The bottom of the screen tells you when:

`Continuing automatically at 3:45pm · Esc to cancel`

So leave the window open. Quit Claude Code and the wait's gone, and resuming the session later won't bring it back.

It still asks for permissions as usual, though, so it can sit on a prompt while you're away.

The limit costs you the wait, not the task.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/interactive-mode (Wait for a usage limit to reset)
