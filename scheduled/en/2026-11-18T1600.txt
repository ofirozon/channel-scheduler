🪟 Claude's halfway through a long refactor, and you want this terminal back.

Don't open another window. Type:

`/bg run the full test suite when you're done`

The session detaches and keeps working with no terminal attached. The text after `/bg` reaches Claude as one last instruction, and you can leave it off.

Later, from any shell:

`claude agents`

Your session's there with the others. Press Enter on its row and you're back in, with a short recap of what happened while you were gone.

If you'd rather keep working here and send a copy off instead, that's `/fork`.

One long task stops costing you a terminal.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/agent-view, code.claude.com/docs/en/commands
