🔀 Two terminals open, and you're the one copy-pasting between them?

Your Claude Code sessions can message each other now. You don't call any tool yourself, you just say what the other session needs:

`Ask the session running in my other terminal whether the migration finished`

Run `/list-agents` to see who's reachable. A session is named after its folder unless you `/rename` it, so rename the ones you'll address often.

It sends text only, never your conversation or your files. Needs v2.1.224 or later, and it's macOS and Linux only.

Worth it because: a finding in one session reaches the other without you retyping it.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/cross-session-messaging
