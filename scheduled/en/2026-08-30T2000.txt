🔑 Locked your API token away from Claude, and broke `gh` doing it?

There's a middle setting, in `~/.claude/settings.json`:

`{`
`  "sandbox": {`
`    "enabled": true,`
`    "network": { "tlsTerminate": {}, "allowedDomains": ["*.github.com"] },`
`    "credentials": {`
`      "envVars": [{ "name": "GH_TOKEN", "mode": "mask", "injectHosts": ["api.github.com"] }]`
`    }`
`  }`
`}`

Sandboxed commands see a fake value. The proxy swaps the real token back in on requests to `api.github.com`, so `gh` still works and its logs never hold the secret. Needs v2.1.199.

Don't skip `tlsTerminate`. Without it the fake value goes out unchanged and auth fails.

Worth it because: your token stays out of everything Claude runs.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/sandboxing
