⏳ Back from lunch, and Claude has been sitting on a question for 40 minutes?

Put this in `~/.claude/settings.json`:

`{ "askUserQuestionTimeout": "5m" }`

The choices are `60s`, `5m` and `10m`, or use the Question auto-continue timeout row in `/config`. When the timer runs out the dialog closes itself, submits whatever you'd already selected, and tells Claude you may be away, so it uses its own judgment and can re-ask later. You get a countdown for the last 20 seconds, and any keypress restarts it.

This won't make a session unattended: permission prompts, plan approval included, never auto-resolve.

Worth it because: a question you didn't see stops costing you the rest of the hour.
