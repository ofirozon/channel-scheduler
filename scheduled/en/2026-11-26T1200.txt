🛑 You told Claude "don't push until I've reviewed it" and went for coffee.

In auto mode that sentence actually binds. The classifier blocks matching actions, even ones its defaults would allow, until you lift it in a later message. Claude deciding the condition was met doesn't count.

There's a hole, though. The boundary isn't saved anywhere. The classifier re-reads it from the transcript, so if compaction drops that message, the boundary goes with it. For anything you'd regret, write it into `.claude/settings.json`:

`"permissions": { "deny": ["Bash(git push *)"] }`

Say it for today. Write it down for always.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/permission-modes
