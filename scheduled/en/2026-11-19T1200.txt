🙈 You type `@logs/` to point Claude at last night's build log, and the picker comes up empty.

It's not broken. The `@` file picker leaves out anything that matches `.gitignore`, and build output usually does. Flip "Respect .gitignore in file picker" off in `/config`, or set it in a settings file:

`"respectGitignore": false`

Expect a noisier picker, since `node_modules` is usually ignored too. If only one repo needs it, put the line in that project's `.claude/settings.local.json` and leave your user settings alone.

Your ignored logs are one `@` away again.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/settings-reference#respectgitignore
