🚦 You leave Claude on a long task, come back an hour later, and it's sat on one permission prompt since minute three.

Claude Code 2.1.319 adds a setting for that. In your settings file:

`"permissions": { "denyInsteadOfPrompting": true }`

Now any call that would wait on a prompt gets denied straight away, Claude is told, and the turn keeps going. Subagents and teammates too, not just `claude -p`.

Your allow rules still decide what runs, so write those first. I'd only switch it on for long unattended runs. On a normal day you want to be asked.

A denied step beats a stalled hour.
