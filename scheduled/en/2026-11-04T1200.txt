🖥️ Full-screen terminal on a wide monitor, and Claude's paragraphs run 200 characters across.

You can cap them. In `~/.claude/settings.json`:

`"maxProseWidth": 80`

Paragraphs, headings, lists and quotes now wrap at 80 columns. Tables and code blocks still get the whole terminal, so a wide diff or a long log line doesn't get chopped. That split is the part I like: readable text, and the stuff that needs the width keeps it.

The value is a whole number of columns, 40 or more. Claude Code ignores anything else, so if nothing changed, check the number. Needs v2.1.282 or later.

Your eyes stop making the long trip back to the left margin.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/settings-reference#maxprosewidth
