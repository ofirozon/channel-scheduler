📧 Claude Code just stopped a shell command to ask about your email address. Read that prompt before you hit yes.

Since v2.1.317, it checks web and shell tool calls and asks first when one is about to send your account email to the network.

Asked for it yourself, say for an API call that needs your address? Allow it. Were you reviewing a PR or summarising a web page when it fired? Deny it and look at what Claude just read. That's what instructions hidden in a page or a README look like when they work.

One extra prompt, and your address doesn't leave quietly.
