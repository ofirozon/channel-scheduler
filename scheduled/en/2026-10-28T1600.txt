🧷 Half your prompt is a pasted JSON blob you no longer want.

In vim mode, press `Esc`, put the cursor on its opening `{` and type:

`d%`

That deletes through the matching `}`, nested braces and all. `c%` does the same and drops you into INSERT, and `y%` yanks it instead. Plain `%` just jumps between the pair. If the cursor isn't on a bracket, it starts from the next one on the line.

It works on `()` and `[]` too. You need v2.1.301 or later, and vim mode switched on in `/config` under Editor mode.

Beats holding `x` and counting.
