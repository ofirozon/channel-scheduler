🐢 Watching Claude think hard about a one-line rename?

Effort level is a separate dial from model choice, and it's the one that decides how long Claude reasons before acting:

    /effort low

Levels are `low`, `medium`, `high`, `xhigh`, `max`. `auto` restores the model default, and no argument opens a slider.

What makes it usable mid-task: it takes effect immediately, without waiting for the current response to finish. Notice a trivial edit turning into a research project, and you can cut it off right there.

Worth it because: cheap tasks stop costing deep-reasoning latency.
