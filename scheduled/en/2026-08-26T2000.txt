🗂️ How much context went to code you didn't write?

`.gitignore` covers most of it: content searches already skip `node_modules/` and `dist/`. The leak is checked-in code, a vendored SDK or committed generated clients. Block it in `.claude/settings.json`:

`{"permissions": {"deny": ["Read(./vendor/**)", "Read(./**/*.generated.*)"]}}`

That stops the built-in file tools, plus `cat`, `head`, `grep` and `find` when a denied path is the argument.

The part that'll surprise you: Claude still sees denied paths in the output of a Bash `grep -r`. The rule blocks opening a file, not scanning past it.

Worth it because: your context window stops filling with code you'll never edit.
