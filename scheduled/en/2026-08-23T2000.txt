⏳ Ever sat waiting for Claude to finish so you could type?

You don't have to. Type your next message and press Enter while Claude is still working: Claude Code queues it and lists it above the input box. Queue it during tool calls and Claude picks it up the moment those finish, inside the same turn.

Changed your mind? Press `Up` from the first line of the input box. Your queued messages come back into the box, one per line, ready to edit or delete.

Worth it because: the thought you had at minute two doesn't wait until minute four.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/interactive-mode
