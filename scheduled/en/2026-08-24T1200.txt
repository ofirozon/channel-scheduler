🎨 Ever described a UI in words and got back the first guess?

Give Claude a brief instead:

`/design a settings panel for a team billing page`

It publishes a canvas of editable artboards to claude.ai and prints the link. Pick the one that's closest, tweak it, then say which option to implement.

Research preview, and artboards ride on artifacts: your session has to be signed in with `/login`. On an API key or Bedrock you get a local file instead of a link.

Requires v2.1.233 or later, on a paid plan.

Worth it because: you choose between layouts side by side instead of talking one into existence.
