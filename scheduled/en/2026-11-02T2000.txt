🧪 Tweaking your app's prompt without an eval means trusting your gut.

If your code calls the Claude API, Claude Code can build the eval for you. In that project, run:

`/claude-api build-eval`

You get an eval set for your Claude-powered app. Once it exists, the next subcommand works against it:

`/claude-api hillclimb`

It improves your app round by round against that eval, and it skips rewordings too small for the eval to measure. Replies cut off at `max_tokens` are marked truncated and counted separately, so they don't quietly drag the average down.

Both need v2.1.259 or later.

After that, "better prompt" is a score, not a feeling.
