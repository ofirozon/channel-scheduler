🔍 Which of Claude's twelve turns actually touched that file?

There's a viewer for that, and it's one command:

`/diff`

Left and right arrows walk your uncommitted changes turn by turn instead of showing one merged blob. Up/down browses files, Enter opens one, Esc goes back. It also refreshes itself when you commit or switch branches in another terminal.

It reads raw git blobs, so a `textconv` filter in `.gitattributes` won't shape the output here.

Worth it because: you find the turn that broke something in seconds, instead of re-reading the whole change.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/commands
