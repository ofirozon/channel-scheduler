🔎 Ever spent an afternoon in twelve tabs to answer one question?

`/deep-research What changed in the Node.js permission model between v20 and v22?`

It's a built-in workflow. Claude fans searches across several angles, cross-checks the sources against each other, and returns one cited report with claims that failed the cross-check already dropped. It runs in the background, so keep working and check `/workflows`.

On Pro, switch Dynamic workflows on in `/config` first.

The catch: it's many agents, not one, so a run costs meaningfully more tokens than just asking. Start narrow.

Worth it because: your answer arrives with its sources checked against each other.
