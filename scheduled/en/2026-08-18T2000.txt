🔄 Enabled skills on claude.ai, then found none of them in your terminal?

They don't come across on their own. One non-interactive run pulls them down:

`CLAUDE_CODE_SYNC_SKILLS=1 claude -p "List the skills you have available"`

That downloads every skill you enabled into `~/.claude/skills/synced/` and exits. Your normal sessions load them from disk afterwards, and `/skills` lists them under `claude.ai sync`.

It's a download, not a live sync. Enable or change a skill on claude.ai later and nothing moves until you run that line again.

Worth it because: the skills you already wrote once on the web stop being web-only.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/skills
