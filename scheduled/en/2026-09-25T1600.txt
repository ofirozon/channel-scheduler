🗝️ Whatever a tool printed in your session is sitting on your disk.

Every message, tool call and result lands in `~/.claude/projects/<project>/<session>.jsonl`, unencrypted, with file permissions the only thing guarding it. Transcripts age out after 30 days by default. The log of every prompt you've typed doesn't age out at all.

See what one project is actually holding:

`claude project purge ~/work/my-repo --dry-run`

That prints the full deletion plan and removes nothing. Drop `--dry-run` and it still asks before it deletes.

I'd rather know what a year of sessions left behind than assume it evaporated.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/claude-directory
