🎨 Tired of retyping the same formatting request every single turn?

Say it once, in a file. Create `~/.claude/output-styles/diagrams-first.md`:

---
name: Diagrams first
keep-coding-instructions: true
---
Start every explanation with a Mermaid diagram, then explain in prose.

Select it in `/config` under Output style. It gets appended to the system prompt, so it shapes every turn from `/clear` onward.

Don't skip `keep-coding-instructions: true`. Without it, your style leaves out Claude Code's built-in software engineering instructions, and you've quietly traded a nicer format for a worse coder.

Worth it because: a preference you retype ten times a day becomes four lines you write once.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/output-styles
