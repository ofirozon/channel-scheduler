🧊 Yesterday's long conversation is the expensive one to pick back up.

The prompt cache expires after a gap, five minutes or an hour depending on the TTL. Resume after that and the first request recomputes the whole conversation.

Since v2.1.304, Pro and Max plans warn you when a resume would take a noticeable share of your 5-hour limit. You get two choices: resume anyway, or start a new conversation.

Most of the time, start new. Claude can look up the old conversation when you refer to it, so you don't lose the thread.

You keep the context you need without paying to reload all of it.

Source: Claude Code v2.1.304 changelog, code.claude.com/docs/en/prompt-caching
