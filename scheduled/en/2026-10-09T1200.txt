⏰ Your 9am code review task just fired at 11pm.

That's Desktop's local scheduled tasks working as designed. They only run while the app is open and the machine is awake. Sleep through a slot and, on wake, Desktop starts exactly one catch-up run for the most recent missed time and drops anything older.

So the prompt has to know what time it is. Something like:

`Only review today's commits. If it's after 5pm, skip the review and just post a summary of what was missed.`

If it really has to happen at 9 with the laptop shut, that's a cloud routine, not a local task.
