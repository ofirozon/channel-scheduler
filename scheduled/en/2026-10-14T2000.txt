🟢 A green run in your routines list tells you less than you'd think.

It means the cloud session started and exited without an infrastructure error. Not whether your task got done.

A blocked network request, a connector tool that wasn't there, a job Claude gave up on halfway: all of that lives inside the run, not in the status dot.

So open each run and read the transcript. And make the prompt say what success looks like, ending with

`Finish with DONE or FAILED and one line on why.`

Then the last line of the transcript tells you what the green dot can't.
