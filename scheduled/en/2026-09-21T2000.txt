🎨 Green means added, red means removed. If you can tell them apart.

Claude Code ships two colorblind-friendly palettes, and they're easy to miss. One line in `~/.claude/settings.json`:

`"theme": "dark-daltonized"`

`light-daltonized` is the light-background twin. It changes the interface colors throughout, diffs included, so the screen you read all day stops resting on a distinction your eyes don't make.

Worth it because: you stop guessing which half of a diff is the part being removed.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/accessibility
