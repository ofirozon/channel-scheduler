🗂️ Does Claude actually read the AGENTS.md your repo already has?

From v2.1.277, yes, with no import and no setting. But only when there's no `CLAUDE.md`, `.claude/CLAUDE.md` or `CLAUDE.local.md` in your working directory or above it. One of those up the tree and your AGENTS.md never loads.

The trap is `CLAUDE.local.md`. Add one for your own uncommitted notes and you've just switched AGENTS.md off for yourself.

Type `/config`, open Project instructions, pick `claude-md-and-agents-md`. Then the file every other agent in your repo reads is the one Claude reads too.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/memory
