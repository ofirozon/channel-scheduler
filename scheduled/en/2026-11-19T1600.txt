💬 The tips that rotate in Claude Code's spinner don't have to be the built-in ones. They can be your team's.

Add them to `.claude/settings.json` and commit it with the repo:

`"spinnerTipsOverride": { "tips": ["Run the integration suite before you push", "Staging deploys freeze at 16:00 on Fridays"] }`

Project settings only take plain strings, though. A custom label, a tips file, or `"excludeDefault": true` to hide the built-in tips all have to live in your user settings or managed settings.

I think it's the one onboarding doc people actually read, because they're staring at it while Claude works.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/settings-reference#spinnertipsoverride
