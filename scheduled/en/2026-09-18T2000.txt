🧭 Two developers, same prompt, different amounts of work come back.

Anthropic studied how people actually run Claude Code sessions. On average you make about 70% of the planning decisions, what to do and which approach, but only 20% of the execution ones: which files, what code, which commands.

The variable is expertise. A novice prompt sets off about five Claude actions and 600 words of output. An expert prompt sets off twelve and 3,200.

Same tool. The difference is how much of the problem you'd settled before you typed.

Worth it because: the study's expert sessions got five times the output per prompt.
