🏢 Claude Code will happily sign in to a personal account on a work laptop.

That session then sits outside your company's org, so your server-managed settings don't reach it, and neither does zero data retention if you have it.

If you admin a Teams or Enterprise org, close the gap in managed settings:

`"forceLoginMethod": "claudeai",`
`"forceLoginOrgUUID": "<your org ID>"`

The ID is in claude.ai admin settings. A claude.ai login to any other org now gets an error, and an `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` in the environment is blocked at startup.

Push the file through your device management tool. Server-managed settings only reach people already signed in to your org, so they can't catch the first login.

Then a work session can only be a work session.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/authentication, code.claude.com/docs/en/zero-data-retention
