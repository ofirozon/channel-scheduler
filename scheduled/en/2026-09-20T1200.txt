🔭 You're refreshing a CI page in another tab while Claude sits idle.

Hand it over. Ask Claude to watch it and a background monitor starts, interjecting when something happens while you keep working:

`Watch the CI run on this PR and tell me when it goes red`

It'll also tail a log file or watch a directory for changes.

Every watch has a deadline, and that's the part nobody expects. Five minutes by default, thirty at most. Claude gets one notice at the end and can restart it, so an unattended watch won't run all afternoon.

The thing you were refreshing tells you itself.
