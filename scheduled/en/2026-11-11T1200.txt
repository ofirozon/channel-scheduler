🌈 "Think hard" isn't a magic phrase in Claude Code. It's just words.

The one keyword it recognizes is `ultrathink`. Put it anywhere in a prompt:

`why does this test only fail on CI? ultrathink`

That asks for deeper reasoning on that turn and leaves your session's effort setting alone. Typed in the terminal on v2.1.311 or later, it can also lift the turn to `xhigh`, and the word turns rainbow when it does.

Stays plain? You probably typed it inside quotes, a path or a command.

Keep the session at its default and spend this on the bug you've already failed to fix twice.
