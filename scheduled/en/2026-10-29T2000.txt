📏 40,000 characters. Past that, Claude only sees the top of your `src/` CLAUDE.md.

Since v2.1.309, a CLAUDE.md in a subdirectory, the files it imports with `@path`, and path-scoped rules each get a size cap when they load on demand. Claude gets the start of the file and a note telling it to Read the rest. Until it does, the rest is out of its context.

The cap is 40,000 characters on a 200K context window, 150,000 on 1M with recent models. Check yours:

`wc -m packages/api/CLAUDE.md`

Over it? Move the tail into `.claude/rules/` files, each with its own cap.

Put the rule you'd hate to lose at the top.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/memory, Claude Code CHANGELOG v2.1.309
