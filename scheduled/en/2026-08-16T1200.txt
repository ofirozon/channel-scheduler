🧹 Ever had Claude summarize the conversation at the worst moment?

By default, compaction waits until the conversation reaches the model's context limit. On a 1M-token model that's a long wait, and every message until then carries the whole history. You can move that line:

`/autocompact 400k`

Anything from 100K to 1M works, and `/autocompact auto` puts it back.

One catch: the command saves to your user settings, so it applies to later sessions too. For a single run, start with `claude --autocompact 400k` instead.

Worth it because: every message pays for the context it carries, so a smaller ceiling is cheaper all day, not just when it compacts.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/model-config
