📜 Don't leave the terminal to find out what an update changed.

Something behaves differently since yesterday? Type:

`/release-notes`

You get a version picker. Pick the release you're on, or show all of them, and the notes print right in your transcript. `claude --version` tells you which one that is.

They don't enter the conversation Claude sees, either. Checking costs you no context and doesn't nudge what Claude does next.

It's the first place to look when a command that worked last week suddenly doesn't.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/commands
