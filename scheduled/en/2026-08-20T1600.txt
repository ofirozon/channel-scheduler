⌨️ Hit Ctrl+S out of pure habit and watched your prompt vanish?

That's `chat:stash` doing its job, and every key in Claude Code is yours to change. Run `/keybindings`, which opens `~/.claude/keybindings.json`:

`{"bindings":[{"context":"Chat","bindings":{"ctrl+s":null}}]}`

`null` unbinds it. Save the file and it applies right away, no restart.

One trap: don't reach for `cmd+`. Most terminals never send that modifier, so the binding quietly never fires. Use `ctrl` or `meta` and it works everywhere.

Worth it because: the shortcut that keeps eating your prompt is one line away from gone.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/keybindings
