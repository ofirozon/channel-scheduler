🐌 Asked for a big parallel sweep and watched your laptop crawl?

Claude Code lets 20 subagents run at once in a session. Past that, the next spawn fails with `Concurrent subagent limit reached`, and Claude is told not to retry rather than to queue. Twenty is generous if your machine is modest.

Set your own ceiling in `~/.claude/settings.json`:

`{ "env": { "CLAUDE_CODE_MAX_CONCURRENT_SUBAGENTS": "5" } }`

Any positive whole number works, and it's read straight from the file, so it applies however you launched `claude`. Needs v2.1.217 or later.

Worth it because: you decide how much of your machine one fan-out can take, instead of finding out at 20.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/sub-agents
