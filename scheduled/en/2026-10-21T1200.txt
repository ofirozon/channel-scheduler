🗜️ If a script sets `CLAUDE_CODE_AUTO_COMPACT_WINDOW=200k`, your sessions have been compacting at 100K.

Before v2.1.304 the variable didn't read suffixes and quietly dropped to the 100K minimum. Now it takes `k` and `M`, and it warns when a value is out of range or ignored.

The bare number is still a trap. This means 200 tokens, not 200,000:

`CLAUDE_CODE_AUTO_COMPACT_WINDOW=200`

`/autocompact 200` means 200,000. The variable doesn't, so it lands on the same 100K floor. Write it out:

`CLAUDE_CODE_AUTO_COMPACT_WINDOW=200000`

It's worth checking, because the variable outranks `/autocompact`, the flag and your settings. A stale export wins every time.

Source: Claude Code v2.1.304 changelog, code.claude.com/docs/en/model-config
