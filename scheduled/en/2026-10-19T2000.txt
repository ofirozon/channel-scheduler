📸 The bug only shows up on your real phone, and the session's on your laptop?

Run `/remote-control` in that session, then open the Claude app on your phone, tap Code and pick the session.

Now attach the screenshot right there. Claude sees the photo as part of your message, and Claude Code also saves it under `~/.claude/uploads/`, so Claude can copy it into the bug report it writes.

The code still runs on your machine, so the laptop has to stay on.

The screenshot goes straight from the phone with the bug to the session that can fix it.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/mobile
