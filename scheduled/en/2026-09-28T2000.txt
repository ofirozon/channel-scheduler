👥 Three Claude sessions reviewing one PR and arguing with each other. That's a real feature you've probably never seen.

Agent teams are experimental and off by default. One entry in `~/.claude/settings.json`:

`"env": { "CLAUDE_CODE_EXPERIMENTAL_AGENT_TEAMS": "1" }`

Then ask: three teammates to review this PR, one of them playing devil's advocate. Each gets its own context window, and they talk to each other, not through you.

It isn't free. While teams are on, any subagent Claude names starts as a teammate, so a team can form unasked, each one a separate Claude instance with its own bill.

I'd switch it on for a review and back to `0` after.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/agent-teams
