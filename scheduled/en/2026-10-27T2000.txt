🧾 The usage tips you keep reading and ignoring now take one key.

Run `/usage` on a Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise plan and the breakdown lists recommendations, one per row:

`25%  Trim ~/code/api/CLAUDE.md · 49k tokens per request`

Since v2.1.305 the footer can show a hint like `f to fix 2 of 3`. Press `f`. A row ending in an `/effort` command gets run for you. For the other rows it can handle, Claude gets one prompt and shows you each change before making it. A row ending in `run /compact` is still yours to type.

No hint at all? Remote Control is connected. Disconnect it and reopen `/usage`.

It's the shortest path from reading the advice to taking it.
