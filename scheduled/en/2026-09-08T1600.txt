🖥️ Ever pasted a wall of terminal output into Slack for a teammate?

Some of it is easier to look at than to read. Ask for a page:

`Make an artifact that walks through this PR with the diff annotated inline.`

Claude publishes it to claude.ai, prints the link, and updates the same page in place as the session continues. `Ctrl+]` reopens the most recent one.

It needs a session signed in with `/login` on a paid plan. On an API key or Bedrock you'll get a local file instead of a link.

Worth it because: a reviewer opens one link instead of rebuilding your reasoning from pasted text.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/artifacts
