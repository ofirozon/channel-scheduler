🧽 Your change works. It's probably also longer than it needed to be.

Before you commit, run:

`/simplify`

It's a bundled skill. Four review agents go over your changed code in parallel: one looks for helpers you rewrote instead of reusing, one for plain simplification, one for efficiency, one for whether the change sits at the right level of abstraction. Then it applies the fixes.

It won't hunt for bugs. That's `/code-review`'s job, and I'd run it second, on the smaller diff.

Commit first, though. It edits your files, and `git diff` is how you pick which cleanups to keep.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/commands
