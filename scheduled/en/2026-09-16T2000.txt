🔔 Long task, you switch tabs, you come back nine minutes late.

Claude Code does fire a notification when it finishes or needs a permission answer. On the default setting that only reaches iTerm2, Ghostty and Kitty; in Warp or the VS Code terminal, nothing. One line in `~/.claude/settings.json` gets you a sound anywhere:

`"preferredNotifChannel": "terminal_bell"`

It rings the bell character, and whether you hear it is your terminal's business. `/terminal-setup` switches Apple Terminal's audible bell off, so if you accepted that prompt you'll want "Audible bell" back on under Settings, Profiles, Advanced.

Worth it because: you notice Claude is waiting in seconds, not on your next glance.
