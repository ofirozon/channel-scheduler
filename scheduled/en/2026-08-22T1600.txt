✏️ Ever sent a prompt with a typo in the word that mattered?

Claude Code can underline misspelled words in the prompt box as you type. It's off by default and wants a checker on your `PATH` first: `aspell`, `hunspell` or `ispell`, whichever you have.

Then add this to `~/.claude/settings.json`:

`{ "spellcheck": { "enabled": true } }`

If nothing ever gets underlined, that's a missing checker, not a clean prompt. `aspell --version` tells you which.

Requires v2.1.235 or later.

Worth it because: you catch the typo before you press Enter, not after Claude goes looking for a file that doesn't exist.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/interactive-mode, "Check spelling as you type"
