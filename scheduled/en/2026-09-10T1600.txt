🕐 Ever done timezone math to match Claude's "done 6:05 PM" to a log line?

That clock at the end of each turn, and every timestamp in the transcript viewer, follows your locale. Your CI logs don't. One key in `~/.claude/settings.json`, v2.1.257 or later:

`"timeFormat": "24-hour-utc"`

Times come out as `18:05Z`. Prefer a real zone? `"timeZone": "Europe/Dublin"` takes any IANA name, but pick a different `timeFormat` first: `24-hour-utc` ignores `timeZone` and stays on UTC.

Worth it because: you read one clock instead of converting between two.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/settings-reference
