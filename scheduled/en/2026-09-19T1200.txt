🎚️ Nine rows in your `/model` picker. How many do you actually pick?

One key in `~/.claude/settings.json` cuts it down to what you use:

`"modelPicker": { "options": [{ "model": "opus", "label": "Deep work" }], "replaceBuiltInOptions": true }`

With that Boolean on the built-in lineup is gone: you get your rows, Default, and whatever the session is already on. Needs v2.1.242 or later.

One trap. The key only works in user or managed settings and is ignored in project and local ones, on purpose, so a repo you clone can't relabel your picker.

Worth it because: `/model` lists the models you actually run instead of a lineup you scroll past.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/settings-reference#modelpicker
