🧭 A new teammate's first week mostly goes on asking how you set Claude Code up.

You can hand them the answer instead. Open the project you know best and run:

`/team-onboarding`

Claude looks at your last 30 days there, the sessions, commands and MCP servers you actually lean on, and writes a markdown guide. Your teammate pastes it as their first message and starts from your setup, not the defaults. On Team and Enterprise plans you also get a share link they can open straight in Claude Code.

Pick a project with real history behind it. The guide is only as good as what you've actually done there.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/commands
