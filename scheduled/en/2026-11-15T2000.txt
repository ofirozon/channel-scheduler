⏳ Stop flipping to the other terminal to see if the migration's done.

Ask the session you're in instead:

`Tell me when the migration session finishes what it's working on`

Claude sets up a one-shot notice on that other session. Watching it costs nothing over there: no turn, no tokens. When it finishes a turn with nothing queued, or exits, a line lands in your session naming it, usually with a one-line status. If your session is idle, Claude starts a new turn and picks it up.

Both sessions need v2.1.236 or later, on the same machine. If nothing arrives in 12 hours, the request expires and Claude is told.

You get to stop being the polling loop.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/cross-session-messaging
