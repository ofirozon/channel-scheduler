🚦 Your CI job went green. One of its plugins never loaded, and the run carried on without it.

Same for a `--mcp-config` server that fails validation: skipped, clean exit, and no warning if your runner captures stderr.

Both land in the `system/init` event, so gate on it:

`claude -p "Fix the lint errors" --output-format stream-json --verbose > run.jsonl`

`jq -e 'select(.type=="system" and .subtype=="init") | ((.plugin_errors // []) + (.mcp_server_errors // [])) == []' run.jsonl`

Any entry in either array and `jq -e` exits non-zero. Leave out `--verbose` and `-p` refuses stream-json.

A red build beats a green one that quietly ran without its tools.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/headless (Fail CI when a plugin or MCP server doesn't load)
