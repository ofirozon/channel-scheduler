🩺 A command runs on your teammate's laptop and refuses on yours?

Check for a privacy variable before you reinstall anything. Claude Code switches some features on through flags it fetches from Anthropic, and `DISABLE_TELEMETRY`, `DO_NOT_TRACK` or `CLAUDE_CODE_DISABLE_NONESSENTIAL_TRAFFIC` stop that fetch. `/skill-doctor`, `/import` and your synced claude.ai skills all go with it.

Since v2.1.326 you don't have to guess. Run:

`/status`

There's a new row that says when your provider or an environment variable is turning features off.

And `DISABLE_TELEMETRY=0` still opts out, so unset it rather than zeroing it.

Thirty seconds, instead of an afternoon diffing dotfiles.

Source: Claude Code CHANGELOG v2.1.326, code.claude.com/docs/en/env-vars (Features that need feature-flag fetching)
