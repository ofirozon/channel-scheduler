🔄 Esc won't stop a `/loop` you gave an interval to.

`/loop 5m check if the deployment finished and tell me what happened`

That reruns the prompt every five minutes while the session's open and idle. Handy. But a fixed-interval loop ignores Esc and Ctrl+C. To end it, ask in plain words:

`cancel the deploy check loop`

Forget, and it keeps firing until the session closes or seven days pass.

Leave the interval out and Claude picks the gap each round. That self-paced version stops with Esc while it's waiting, and Claude can end it on its own once the job's done. For most polling I'd skip the interval.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/scheduled-tasks
