🔄 You updated Claude Code this morning. Your background sessions are still on the old build.

A session that was already running doesn't pick up the new binary until it restarts. You don't have to stop and redispatch each one:

`claude respawn --all`

That restarts every running session, and any session with a saved conversation resumes it, so nothing loses its place. A session-only `/model` switch survives the respawn too.

For a single session it's `claude respawn <id>`.

I'd make it the second half of every update. Otherwise the fix you updated for never reaches the sessions doing the work.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/agent-view
