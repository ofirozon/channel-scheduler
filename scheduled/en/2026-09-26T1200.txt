🫥 You paste an issue into the prompt. Part of it is invisible.

Unicode has characters that draw nothing at all: tag characters, invisible formatting marks. They survive a copy out of a web page or a PDF, your terminal shows you nothing, and to a model they're text like any other text.

From v2.1.277, Claude Code strips them out of your prompt and shows you the cleaned version for review before it's sent.

So read that line when it turns up. It's telling you the thing you pasted wasn't the thing you saw.
