🔑 My cron jobs went quiet three times this summer. Each time, the claude.ai login behind them had expired.

Since v2.1.318 you can see the date coming. `claude auth status` now includes `loginExpiresAt`, so a script can check it:

`claude auth status | jq -r .loginExpiresAt`

And `claude -p` starts printing a one-line warning to stderr 10 days before a stored sign-in ends. That only helps if your job's stderr lands somewhere you actually read, so point cron's output at a log you open.

Ten days of notice beats finding out from a dead job.

Source: Claude Code changelog, v2.1.318
