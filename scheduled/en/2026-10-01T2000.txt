🌙 Ten retries, then your nightly job goes red.

That's the default, and it's the right call when you're sitting there watching the terminal. At 3am it isn't.

In CI, set this before the run:

`CLAUDE_CODE_RETRY_WATCHDOG=1`

429 and 529 capacity errors then retry until they clear instead of giving up. Other transient failures get 300 attempts instead of 10, roughly three hours of backoff, so set a job timeout you can live with.

A 429 that reports a spend limit still fails on the spot, and it should.

Your scheduled run stops going red for reasons that have nothing to do with your code.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/errors
