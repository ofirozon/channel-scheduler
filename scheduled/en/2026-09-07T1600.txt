🧹 How many of your loaded skills have you never once used?

Each one costs context before you type anything. Run:

`/skill-doctor`

It reports what your own skills cost and how often each gets used, flags the never-invoked ones, and says where to switch them off. Plugins you haven't touched lately, too. Interactively the report opens in the `/plugin` manager's Stats tab; under `claude -p` it prints as plain text.

One catch: it won't run over Remote Control from a phone or browser, only in the terminal on that session's own machine. Needs v2.1.252 or later.

Worth it because: you can't prune context you've never measured.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/slash-commands
