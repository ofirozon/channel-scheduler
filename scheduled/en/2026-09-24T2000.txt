⌨️ Three messages queued, and when the turn ends only one of them goes.

That's the actual rule: a turn that finishes with a queue behind it sends the oldest message next, and the others keep waiting their turn.

`Esc` is the existing way out. It interrupts, and Claude Code sends what you queued right away. From v2.1.275 there's a key that does it without reaching for the stop button:

`Ctrl+Enter`, and `Ctrl+X Ctrl+S` does the same thing.

It sends the whole queue in one go, and everything stays gray until the model actually has it.

Source: Claude Code CHANGELOG v2.1.275, code.claude.com/docs/en/interactive-mode
