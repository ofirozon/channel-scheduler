🧠 Watched a session try the same broken fix four times?

Give it someone to ask:

`claude --advisor opus`

Claude decides when to consult it: before committing to an approach, when an error keeps recurring, before calling a task done. The advisor reads the whole conversation, and `Ctrl+O` on the `Advising` line shows you what it said back. `/advisor opus` saves it as your default. Experimental, and Anthropic API only.

It isn't free: every consultation bills at the advisor's rates and counts toward your plan limits.

Worth it because: Opus judgment lands at the three moments that decide the outcome, not on all forty tool calls.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/advisor
