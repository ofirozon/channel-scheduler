📸 Still typing out what the broken screen looks like?

Copy the screenshot and paste it straight into the prompt:

`Ctrl+V`

Then ask "here's the error, what's causing it?" and Claude reads the picture: the stack trace in the corner, the button that's two pixels off. No clipboard? Give it a path instead: "Analyze this image: ~/Desktop/bug.png".

The part that trips up Mac users: `Cmd+V` only works in iTerm2. Everywhere else on macOS it's `Ctrl+V`, and yes, that feels wrong for the first week.

Worth it because: a screenshot carries the detail you'd never think to describe.
