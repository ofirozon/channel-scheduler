🧩 Two repos, two `verify` skills. Before v2.1.309, you couldn't reach the second one.

Say you work in your app and pull in the CLI repo:

`claude --add-dir ../cli`

Now the clashing skill from the added folder gets a prefix instead of going dark. Your project keeps the short name:

`/verify` runs the app's
`/cli:verify` runs the one from `../cli`

The prefix is the added folder's name, and the `/` menu lists it, so there's nothing to guess. Add two folders that both ship a `verify` and the first one you added keeps the short name.

You can stop renaming everything `verify-cli` just to dodge a collision.

Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/skills, Claude Code CHANGELOG v2.1.309
